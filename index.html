About Us



At Grill & Go, we believe that great food brings people together. Our passion is creating dishes that blend fresh, local ingredients with a touch of creativity — from juicy burgers to handcrafted specialties that make every bite unforgettable. Whether you're stopping by for a quick meal, a family dinner, or a night out with friends, we promise you a warm welcome, excellent service, and flavors that feel like home. Fresh. Flavorful. Made with love.

Our Menu

  • Hotdog Deluxe

    A hearty twist on a classic favorite! Juicy grilled sausage served in a soft bun, topped with a perfectly fried egg, crisp fresh salad, and our signature sauce. A perfect balance of flavor, texture, and satisfaction in every bite.

  • Cheese Burger

    he timeless favorite done right. A juicy beef patty topped with melted cheddar cheese, fresh lettuce, ripe tomatoes, and our house-made sauce, all nestled in a soft toasted bun. Simple, flavorful, and irresistibly satisfying.

  • Peperonni Pizza

    A crispy, golden crust layered with rich tomato sauce, gooey mozzarella cheese, and topped with perfectly spiced pepperoni slices. Every bite delivers the perfect balance of crunch, melt, and flavor — a true classic for pizza lovers..

  • Burritos

    Soft flour tortilla stuffed with savory seasoned meat, melted cheese, fresh lettuce, and creamy sauce — rolled tight and grilled for the perfect bite every time. A flavorful fusion of comfort and spice!

  • Phillisteak Sandwitch

    Tender slices of grilled beef, melted cheese, and sautéed onions packed into a soft toasted roll. Juicy, cheesy, and bursting with flavor — a true classic you can’t resist!

  • French Tacos

    A delicious French-style taco packed with tender meat, crispy fries, melted cheese, and our signature homemade sauce — all wrapped in a perfectly grilled tortilla. A unique mix of textures and flavors that every street-food lover will crave!
